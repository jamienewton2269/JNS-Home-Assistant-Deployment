Help with accounts and recovery

Your username is the short directory name, such as jamie. Email addresses used by applications can be mapped to the same identity during service integration.

Choose a unique passphrase of at least 14 characters. Recovery uses the address registered by an administrator; the form cannot redirect a reset link to an arbitrary address.

Directory administrator accounts use a separate recovery procedure. If you cannot access your registered recovery email, contact your administrator.

Services must be connected to this directory before they share its password. Existing service logins are migrated individually.
